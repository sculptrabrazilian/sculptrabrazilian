Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Sculptra BBL and how does it work?

A Sculptra BBL is a non-surgical procedure that uses injections of poly-L-lactic acid (Sculptra) into the buttocks area to stimulate your body's own collagen production. Over the weeks and months following treatment, new collagen builds up naturally, adding volume, shape and lift to the buttocks without any surgery.

How long do Sculptra butt lift results last?

Results from a Sculptra Brazilian butt lift typically last two to five years, making it one of the longest-lasting non-surgical options available. The duration depends on factors such as your metabolism, lifestyle and the number of treatment sessions completed.

Is the non-surgical BBL with Sculptra safe?

Yes. Sculptra is FDA-approved and has been used safely in aesthetic medicine for over 20 years. Our trained medical professionals perform every treatment under careful clinical protocols. Most clients experience only mild, temporary side effects such as slight swelling or bruising at the injection sites.

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients achieve their desired outcome with two to four sessions spaced six to eight weeks apart. During your consultation at our Miami medical spa, we will assess your individual goals and create a personalized treatment plan outlining the recommended number of sessions.

Is there downtime after a Sculptra butt lift?

There is minimal downtime. You may experience slight swelling or bruising at the injection sites for a few days. Most clients return to regular activities the same day or the next day. We recommend avoiding strenuous exercise and prolonged sitting for 24 to 48 hours after treatment.

Who is a good candidate for Sculptra injections for butt enhancement?

Good candidates are adults in general good health who want to add volume, improve shape or address asymmetry in the buttocks without surgery. Sculptra is particularly well-suited for those who want gradual, natural-looking results and are willing to complete a series of sessions for optimal outcome.
